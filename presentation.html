Section 1: Problem Statement

The Pain of Modern Travel Planning

Why travelers struggle to explore diverse states like Karnataka

⏳

10–15 Hours Wasted

Travelers manually juggle 5+ tabs: KSRTC bus schedules, Google Maps, hotel aggregators, TripAdvisor reviews, and dated travel blogs.

💸

Severe Budget Overshoot

Hidden taxi surcharges, inaccurate seasonal stay rates, and unpredictable meal expenses frequently cause 30–50% budget overruns.

📍

Impossible Routes & Traps

Generic guides suggest unfeasible routes (e.g. Hampi to Coorg in a 2-day trip) and dump nature-lovers into crowded city commercial bazaars.

Target Impact Group: Domestic & International Tourists, Solo Backpackers (₹5k budget), Families, and Luxury Seekers across all 31 Karnataka districts.

Section 3: How Your Solution Is Different

What Makes Us Radically Better

Four core pillars of our differentiated intelligence

🧠 01. Natural Language Trip Planner

Type freely: "I have ₹5,000 and 5 days. Waterfalls only starting tomorrow from Bengaluru." The AI extracts budget, duration, departure dates, interests, and transit mode automatically.

👑 02. Verified Properties & Real Tiers

Selecting Luxury assigns authentic 5-Star Heritage Palaces (The Leela Palace, Lalitha Mahal Palace, Evolve Back Hampi). Budget assigns certified Zostel dorms and KSRTC Sarige Express buses.

🛡️ 03. Strict Interest & Mode Isolation

Guaranteed zero contamination. When "Waterfalls" is selected, only Jog, Hebbe, Abbey, and Jhari cascades appear. When "Only Cab" is selected, zero buses appear.

🗓️ 04. Calendar Sync & Bilingual Kannada

Calculates real calendar dates (e.g. Tue, 20 Oct – Fri, 23 Oct 2026) paired with authentic Kannada transliterations and native Kannada numerals (ಮಂಗಳವಾರ, ೨೦ ಅಕ್ಟೋಬರ್ ೨೦೨೬).

⭐ Live demo verified: 100% adherence to user constraints with sub-second response times.

Why our approach differs from standard AI projects:
Standard projects send prompts to a remote LLM API that invents fake prices and random routes. Our approach uses a deterministic knowledge engine with pre-verified transit rates, real property databases, and topological graph traversal — delivering instant, 100% reliable results with zero API costs.
